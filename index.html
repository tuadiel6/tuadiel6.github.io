I am a Software Engineer and Researcher with a PhD in Computer Science, specializing in Software Engineering.

My doctoral research at the Gran Sasso Science Institute (GSSI) focused on Model-Driven Engineering, particularly the automated generation of REST APIs for model management, model co-evolution, and interoperability across heterogeneous technical spaces.

Currently, I work at Fondazione Gran Sasso Tech (GST), working on MATISSE, a European research project that focuses on Digital Twin Engineering to support the development of innovative solutions for early verification and validation of industrial systems.

What I work on

Research

I study how models can drive real software: generating the services around them, keeping them in step as they evolve, and connecting them to the tools and systems people already use.

Current project 2025 – now · Fondazione Gran Sasso Tech

MATISSE: Digital Twins for industrial systems

A European research project exploring how Digital Twins can support the design, simulation and validation of industrial systems. I lead project tasks and develop Digital Twin solutions for system monitoring, simulation, validation and verification.

Research themes

REST APIs for model management

Generating REST APIs automatically from metamodels, and provisioning them on the fly, so that models can be created, queried and updated by any web client without hand-written services.

Papers: MODELS 2024, ICWS 2024

Model co-evolution and interoperability

Keeping models and the artefacts built on them consistent as metamodels change, and moving models across heterogeneous technical spaces such as EMF and Linked Open Data.

Papers: MODELS-C 2023, MDE for Smart IoT workshop 2022

Model-based workflow automation

Bridging workflow automation tools with modelling ecosystems, and building model-based assistants that help users design and run automated workflows.

Papers: SEAA 2024, MODELS-C 2023

Digital Twins and software architecture

Applying model-driven techniques to Digital Twins of industrial systems, and studying quality metrics that help assess software architectures.

Papers: ICSA 2023 · Project: MATISSE
